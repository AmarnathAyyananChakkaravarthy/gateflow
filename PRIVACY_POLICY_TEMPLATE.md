## Introduction

Welcome to GateFlow ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application GateFlow (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

GateFlow collects and stores the following personal information locally on your device:

- **Student Identification Numbers:** Used for verifying student identity at gates
- **Access Timestamps:** Date and time of gate access events
- **Access Type:** Whether the access was an entry or exit
- **Student Information:** Name, department, year, roll number (as stored in local database)

### 1.2 Technical Information

- **Device Information:** We do not collect device information
- **Log Data:** Access logs are stored locally and not transmitted

### 1.3 Location Data

GateFlow does **not** collect, store, or transmit any location data.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

### 2.1 App Functionality
- Verify student identity at security gates
- Track entry and exit events
- Maintain security audit trails
- Generate access reports via CSV export

### 2.2 Local Storage Only
All data is stored **locally on your device** using SQLite database. We do not:
- Transmit data to external servers
- Store data in the cloud
- Sync data across devices
- Share data with third parties

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local Storage
- All student data and access logs are stored locally on your device
- Data is stored using standard iOS/Android SQLite encryption
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No data is stored on external servers

### 3.2 Data Retention
- Access logs are automatically deleted after **90 days**
- Student database persists until app is uninstalled
- You can manually delete all data by uninstalling the app
- Deleted data cannot be recovered

### 3.3 Security Measures
- Data stored using device's standard SQLite encryption
- No network transmission of personal data
- Standard mobile OS security measures apply
- Camera access only used for QR code scanning

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 Third-Party Sharing
We do **NOT**:
- Share data with third parties
- Sell your data
- Rent your data
- Transmit your data to external services
- Use analytics or tracking services

### 4.2 CSV Export
The only way data leaves your device is through:
- **Manual CSV export:** You can export access logs to CSV format
- **User-initiated sharing:** You control where exported files are shared
- No automatic sharing or synchronization occurs

---

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

### 5.1 Access Your Data
- View all access logs in the "Access Logs" screen
- Export your data via CSV export feature

### 5.2 Delete Your Data
- Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data
- Individual log entries cannot be selectively deleted
- Logs are automatically purged after 90 days

### 5.3 Opt-Out
- As all data is local-only, there is no opt-out needed for data sharing
- You can stop using the app at any time

## 7. Permissions

- **Purpose:** Scan QR codes for student verification
